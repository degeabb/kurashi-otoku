Step-by-step guide

Employee Referral Program

Rakuten Mobile's employee referral campaign is open to everyone: apply through a Rakuten Group employee's referral link and get up to 14,000 Rakuten Points. This guide walks you through the application using real screenshots.

14,000 pt

Switching from another carrier and keeping your number (MNP)

13,000 pt

Any other sign-up: new number, additional line, or re-contract

Conditions:
  • Log in to the referral link with your Rakuten ID before you apply (if you forget, logging in within 7 days of applying, including the application day, still counts)
  • Apply for Rakuten SAIKYO Plan (or SAIKYO U-NEXT) and start using it by the end of the second month after the referral login
  • Make a 10+ second call from the Rakuten Link app by the same deadline
  • Up to 5 lines per person; Rakuten Group employees themselves are not eligible. Amounts apply to applications from Sept 17, 2026

The screenshots show the Japanese application screens as of November 2026 (personal details are blurred). Screens may change slightly when Rakuten updates its site.

1

Open the referral link and sign in

Tap any “Apply” button on this site to open the employee referral page, then sign in with your Rakuten ID (email or username).

No Rakuten ID yet?: Tap “Create a new Rakuten account (free)” to register — see our Rakuten account registration guide.
Important: Always sign in through the referral link first. If you open the Rakuten Mobile site directly and apply, the referral is not recorded.
2

Enter your password

Enter your Rakuten password and tap Next.

3

Add your member information (first time only)

If your Rakuten ID is new, you'll be asked for your gender and date of birth. Enter them exactly as on your ID document — they can't be changed later.

4

Choose your plan

Select “Rakuten SAIKYO Plan” and tap “Start with Plan” (Japanese screen: 「プランを申し込む」). To buy a phone at the same time, use the “Apply for plan only or with a phone” link above the button. You can check or change physical SIM / eSIM under SIM TYPE in the shopping cart.

eSIM tip: With eSIM you can activate without waiting for a SIM card to be delivered. Check that your phone supports eSIM first.
5

Verify your identity

The easiest option is “スマホでかんたん本人確認”: scan your ID's IC chip with your phone in the my Rakuten Mobile app (inside Rakuten Link). Accepted photo IDs: My Number card or driver's license (Japanese nationals); Residence Card (在留カード) or Special Permanent Resident Certificate (foreign residents). You can also mail a copy of your residence certificate (住民票), but it takes longer.

Check your details: Your name and address must match your ID exactly, or the verification will be sent back.
6

Choose your phone number

Keep your number (MNP): enter the number you want to transfer. New number: pick one of three suggested numbers (free), or pay ¥1,100 to choose the last 4 digits.

Switching carriers (MNP): If your current carrier supports MNP One-Stop, you don't need an MNP reservation number — just have your current carrier's login ID and password ready. Otherwise, get a reservation number beforehand. The contract holder's name at your current carrier must match your Rakuten Mobile application.
7

Delivery, payment and options

Confirm your delivery address (it must match your ID; delivery usually takes 1–3 days) and payment method (credit card or bank account transfer), then tap “この内容で申し込む” (apply).

If you don't add the 15-minute call option (¥1,100/month) or voicemail (¥330/month), a reminder pops up. If you don't need them, tap “加入せずに次へ” (continue without adding).

Save money: Domestic calls through the Rakuten Link app are free, so most people don't need a call option.
8

Start using your line and call with Rakuten Link

When your SIM arrives (or your eSIM is ready), activate the line and make a call of 10+ seconds from the Rakuten Link app — both by the end of the second month after your referral login. You can check your call time in my Rakuten Mobile under Usage → Calls → Rakuten Link (domestic).

  • Points arrive in 3 installments, around the end of the 4th, 5th and 6th month after the referral login month (MNP: 5,000 / 5,000 / 4,000; other: 5,000 / 4,000 / 4,000)
  • They are limited-time points, valid for 6 months including the day they are granted
  • Calls to 0570 numbers and similar paid connection services don't count

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know a Rakuten employee?
You need a Rakuten Group employee's referral link. The apply buttons on this site use the site owner's (a Rakuten Group employee's) referral link, so you can apply directly from here.
I already use Rakuten Mobile. Can I still get points?
Yes, for a new additional line (2nd line or later, up to 5 lines per person). People who cancelled before and sign up again are also eligible.
I applied without opening the referral link first. What now?
Log in via the referral link within 7 days of applying (including the application day). Rakuten may ask for evidence that you simply forgot.
Can I combine this with other campaigns?
Only with the campaigns listed in the official rules (for example, some iPhone discounts). Check the campaign rules (campaign code 2162) before applying.
What if I cancel soon after joining?
Points may not be granted if you never start using the line, have unpaid bills, or Rakuten judges the contract was only for the points (for example, repeated short-term cancellations).
